The Painters drawer lists the painters themselves, each with a Fly to button that takes the map to where they were last seen.

- Live sessions come first, painters before browsers, then anyone known only by a claim; you are always at the top with an Edit for your regions.
- Fly to goes to drafted pixels, else the viewport, else the newest claim. It reads presence again on click, so someone who just left gets a message instead of a stale flight.
