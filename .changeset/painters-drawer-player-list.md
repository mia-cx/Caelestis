The Painters drawer lists who is online instead of region claims, each with a Fly to button that takes the map to their viewport.

- Painters come first, then browsers, then anyone who has not shared a viewport; those have no Fly to.
- Fly to reads presence again on click, so someone who just left gets a message instead of a stale flight.
- The drawer's Edit button is gone with the claim list; press M and use the select tool to edit your regions.
