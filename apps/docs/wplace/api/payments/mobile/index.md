---
title: Mobile app payments
description: Google Play and App Store purchase checks used by the Wplace mobile apps.
---

Only the Android and iOS apps call these.

- [[Get-play-account-id|Get my Google Play account id]]: Get the id the Android app attaches to Google Play purchases.
- [[Verify-play-purchase|Verify a Google Play purchase]]: Credit a Google Play purchase made in the Android app.
- [[Verify-pending-play-purchases|Verify pending Play purchases]]: Credit any Google Play purchases that weren't credited yet.
- [[Get-play-claim|Get a Play claim]]: Check whether one Google Play purchase was credited.
- [[List-play-history|List Google Play purchases]]: List the signed-in player's Google Play purchases.
- [[Get-app-store-token|Get an App Store token]]: Get the token the iOS app attaches to App Store purchases.
- [[Get-app-store-consent|Get App Store consent]]: Read whether the player agreed to share consumption data with Apple.
- [[Set-app-store-consent|Set App Store consent]]: Save whether the player agrees to share consumption data with Apple.
- [[Verify-app-store-purchase|Verify an App Store purchase]]: Credit an App Store purchase made in the iOS app.
