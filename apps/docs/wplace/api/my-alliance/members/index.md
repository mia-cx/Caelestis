Membership management for the player's own alliance.

- [[List-own-alliance-members|List my alliance's members]]: List members of the player's own alliance, with the fields only members see.
- [[List-alliance-bans|List my alliance's bans]]: List players banned from the player's alliance.
- [[Kick-alliance-member|Kick a member]]: Remove a member from the player's alliance.
- [[Ban-from-alliance|Ban a player from my alliance]]: Ban a player from the alliance so they can't rejoin.
- [[Unban-from-alliance|Unban a player from my alliance]]: Lift an alliance ban.
- [[Promote-alliance-admin|Make a member admin]]: Promote an alliance member to admin.
- [[Demote-alliance-admin|Remove an admin]]: Demote an alliance admin back to member.
- [[Set-alliance-member-role|Set a member's role]]: Give an alliance member a different role.
- [[Get-alliance-role-permissions|Get role permissions]]: Read what each role may do in the player's alliance.
- [[Set-alliance-role-permissions|Set role permissions]]: Change what each role may do in the player's alliance.
- [[Transfer-alliance-leadership|Hand over leadership]]: Make another member the alliance leader.
- [[List-alliance-invites|List invite links]]: List the alliance's active invite links.
- [[Create-alliance-invite|Create an invite link]]: Create an alliance invite link with an expiry and an optional use limit.
- [[Revoke-alliance-invite|Revoke an invite link]]: Disable one alliance invite link.
- [[Join-alliance-by-invite|Join with an invite link]]: Join an alliance through an invite link code.
- [[List-join-requests|List join requests]]: List players waiting for approval to join the alliance.
- [[Review-join-request|Accept or reject a join request]]: Accept or reject one player's request to join the alliance.
- [[Cancel-join-request|Cancel my join request]]: Withdraw the signed-in player's own pending request to join an alliance.
