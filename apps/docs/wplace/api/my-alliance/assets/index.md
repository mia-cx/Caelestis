---
title: Alliance pictures and banners
description: Draft, paint and publish an alliance's pixel-art picture and banner.
---

An alliance's picture and banner are painted pixel by pixel in drafts, then published as versions.

- [[Get-alliance-assets|List alliance asset versions]]: List the drafts and published versions of the alliance picture, banner or canvas.
- [[Create-alliance-asset-draft|Start an asset draft]]: Start a new draft of an alliance picture or banner, optionally from a version.
- [[Get-alliance-asset-canvas|Get a draft canvas]]: Get the pixels or the revision of an alliance asset draft.
- [[Get-alliance-asset-draft-image|Get a draft image]]: Download an alliance asset draft as an image.
- [[Paint-alliance-asset|Paint an asset draft]]: The request the client sends to paint pixels on an alliance picture or banner draft.
- [[Finish-alliance-asset-draft|Finish an asset draft]]: Close a draft, and optionally publish it as the alliance's picture or banner.
- [[Select-alliance-asset-version|Use an asset version]]: Make one saved version the alliance's current picture or banner.
- [[Unpublish-alliance-asset|Remove a published asset]]: Stop showing the alliance's current picture or banner.
- [[Delete-alliance-asset-version|Delete an asset version]]: Delete a saved alliance asset version that is not in use.
- [[Get-alliance-asset-version-image|Get an asset version image]]: Download a saved alliance asset version as an image.
- [[List-alliance-asset-editors|List draft editors]]: List who may paint on an alliance asset draft.
- [[Add-alliance-asset-editor|Add a draft editor]]: Let another member paint on an alliance asset draft.
- [[Remove-alliance-asset-editor|Remove a draft editor]]: Stop a member from painting on an alliance asset draft.
- [[Copy-alliance-asset-editors|Copy draft editors]]: Give a draft the same editors as another draft.
