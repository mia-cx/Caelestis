title: Alliance templates
description: Shared image templates that alliance leaders place on the map for members.

Alliance templates are image overlays that leaders share with chosen members.

- [[List-alliance-templates|List alliance templates]]: List the templates the player's alliance shares, with storage use.
- [[Create-alliance-template|Upload an alliance template]]: Upload a new template image for the alliance, with its audience and placement.
- [[Replace-alliance-template|Replace an alliance template]]: Replace a template's image and settings.
- [[Update-alliance-template|Update template details]]: Change a template's name, audience or display settings without a new image.
- [[Place-alliance-template|Move an alliance template]]: Change where on the map an alliance template sits.
- [[Delete-alliance-template|Delete an alliance template]]: Delete one of the alliance's templates.
- [[Get-alliance-template-image|Get a template image]]: Download an alliance template's full image.
- [[Get-alliance-template-thumbnail|Get a template thumbnail]]: Download a small preview of an alliance template.
