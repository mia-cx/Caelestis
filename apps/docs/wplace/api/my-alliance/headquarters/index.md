title: Alliance headquarters

---

A headquarters is a separate square canvas, anchored at a spot on the world map, that only alliance members paint. Public reads are under [Alliances](../../alliances/).

- [[Get-own-headquarters|Get my alliance's headquarters]]: Get the player's own alliance headquarters, with member-only details.
- [[Get-own-headquarters-manifest|Get my headquarters tile manifest]]: List the tiles of the player's own alliance headquarters canvas with their versions, including a private headquarters.
- [[Get-own-headquarters-snapshot|Get my headquarters snapshot]]: Download the changed tiles of an area of the player's own alliance headquarters in one binary response.
- [[Get-own-headquarters-canvas|Get my headquarters canvas]]: Get the tile manifest of the player's own headquarters, including private ones.
- [[Get-own-headquarters-image|Get my headquarters image]]: Download the player's own alliance headquarters as one image.
- [[Get-headquarters-pixel|Get a headquarters pixel]]: Get who painted one pixel of the player's alliance headquarters.
- [[Set-headquarters-location|Move my headquarters]]: Set where the alliance headquarters is anchored on the world map.
- [[Unlock-headquarters|Unlock a headquarters]]: Buy the alliance its headquarters canvas with alliance coins.
- [[Expand-headquarters|Expand my headquarters]]: Grow the alliance headquarters canvas to a larger size.
- [[Paint-headquarters|Paint the headquarters]]: The request the client sends to paint pixels on the alliance headquarters canvas.
- [[List-headquarters-timeouts|List headquarters timeouts]]: List members currently barred from painting the alliance headquarters.
- [[Create-headquarters-timeout|Time out a member]]: Bar a member from painting the headquarters for a while, with a reason.
- [[Revoke-headquarters-timeout|Lift a headquarters timeout]]: End a member's headquarters timeout early, with a reason.
