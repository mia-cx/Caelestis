Droplets are Wplace's in-game currency. These endpoints list and buy items with droplets. Buying droplets with money is under [Payments](../payments/).

- [[Get-store-catalog|Get the store catalogue]]: List every product in the store, with prices and what each grants.
- [[Get-store-offers|Get store offers]]: List current store promotions and each payment provider's prices.
- [[Get-my-store-catalog|Get my store state]]: Get what the signed-in player owns and may still buy in the store.
- [[Buy-catalog-product|Buy a product with droplets]]: Buy a store product with droplets at the price the player saw.
- [[Purchase-item|Buy a colour or flag]]: Buy a premium colour, a flag or another droplet item.
- [[List-store-frames|List frames for sale]]: List the profile frames the signed-in player can buy.
- [[Buy-frame|Buy a frame]]: Buy a profile frame with droplets.
- [[List-store-name-cosmetics|List name cosmetics for sale]]: List the name fonts and styles the signed-in player can buy.
- [[Buy-name-cosmetic|Buy a name cosmetic]]: Buy a name font or style with droplets.
- [[Get-gift-recipient|Look up a gift recipient]]: Check that a player can receive a store gift before paying.
