---
title: "Staff: players"
description: Staff-only routes for player accounts, punishments and grants.
---

These routes serve Wplace's staff dashboard, which ships inside the public client. They are listed so mod authors can recognise them in the bundle. Players get `401` or `403`. Their notes describe what each route is for, and nothing more.

- [[Staff-Search-players|Search players (staff)]]: Staff-only route that searches player accounts by name or id.
- [[Staff-Get-a-player-account|Get a player's account (staff)]]: Staff-only route that shows one player's full account record.
- [[Staff-Get-several-accounts|Get several accounts (staff)]]: Staff-only route that shows several players' account records at once.
- [[Staff-Edit-a-player-account|Change a player's cosmetics (staff)]]: Staff-only route that grants, removes or refunds one player's badges and cosmetics.
- [[Staff-Sign-a-player-out|Sign a player out (staff)]]: Staff-only route that ends every session of one player.
- [[Staff-Get-a-player-anti-cheat-record|Get a player's anti-cheat record (staff)]]: Staff-only route that shows one player's anti-cheat signals and score.
- [[Staff-List-a-player-appeals|List a player's appeals (staff)]]: Staff-only route that lists the ban appeals one player sent.
- [[Staff-List-a-player-punishments|List a player's punishments (staff)]]: Staff-only route that lists one player's bans and timeouts.
- [[Staff-Get-a-punishment-ticket|Get a punishment ticket (staff)]]: Staff-only route that shows the ticket behind one punishment.
- [[Staff-Overturn-a-punishment|Overturn a punishment (staff)]]: Staff-only route that reverses a ban or timeout.
- [[Staff-Review-an-automatic-ban|Review an automatic ban (staff)]]: Staff-only route that confirms or rejects a ban the anti-cheat issued.
- [[Staff-List-a-player-tickets|List a player's tickets (staff)]]: Staff-only route that lists reports filed by or against one player.
- [[Staff-Get-a-player-ticket-stats|Get a player's ticket stats (staff)]]: Staff-only route that counts one player's reports by outcome.
- [[Staff-List-a-player-purchases|List a player's purchases (staff)]]: Staff-only route that lists one player's store purchases.
- [[Staff-List-a-player-gifts|List a player's gifts (staff)]]: Staff-only route that lists store gifts one player sent or got.
- [[Staff-Give-a-player-a-gift|Give a player a gift (staff)]]: Staff-only route that grants a store product to a player.
- [[Staff-List-giftable-products|List giftable products (staff)]]: Staff-only route that lists products staff may grant to a player.
- [[Staff-List-a-player-cosmetics|List a player's cosmetics (staff)]]: Staff-only route that lists the frames, badges and name styles a player owns.
- [[Staff-List-grantable-cosmetics|List grantable cosmetics (staff)]]: Staff-only route that lists every cosmetic staff may grant.
- [[Staff-List-a-player-pictures|List a player's pictures (staff)]]: Staff-only route that lists every profile picture one player made.
- [[Staff-Remove-a-player-picture|Remove a player's picture (staff)]]: Staff-only route that removes a profile picture that breaks the rules.
- [[Staff-Reroll-a-player-avatar|Reroll a player's avatar (staff)]]: Staff-only route that gives a player a new generated avatar.
- [[Staff-Rename-a-player|Rename a player (staff)]]: Staff-only route that changes a player's display name.
- [[Staff-Change-a-player-email|Change a player's email (staff)]]: Staff-only route that changes the email on a player's account.
- [[Staff-Reset-phone-verification|Reset phone verification (staff)]]: Staff-only route that changes a player's phone verification state.
- [[Staff-Adjust-droplets|Adjust droplets (staff)]]: Staff-only route that adds or removes droplets on an account.
- [[Staff-Adjust-prism|Adjust prism (staff)]]: Staff-only route that adds or removes prism on an account.
- [[Staff-Get-player-notes|Get player notes (staff)]]: Staff-only route that shows staff notes on one player.
- [[Staff-Add-a-player-note|Add a player note (staff)]]: Staff-only route that adds a staff note to one player from the staff dashboard.
- [[Staff-Ban-a-player|Ban a player (staff)]]: Staff-only route that bans a player from Wplace.
- [[Staff-Unban-a-player|Unban a player (staff)]]: Staff-only route that lifts a Wplace ban.
- [[Staff-Time-out-a-player|Time out a player (staff)]]: Staff-only route that stops a player painting for a while.
- [[Staff-Grant-droplets-from-the-summary|Grant droplets from the summary (staff)]]: Staff-only route that adds droplets from the staff summary page.
- [[Staff-Require-phone-verification|Require phone verification (staff)]]: Staff-only route that requires phone verification from a player.
- [[Staff-Reload-the-punishment-cache|Reload the punishment cache (staff)]]: Staff-only route that reloads the server's cached punishments.
- [[Staff-Get-staff-permissions|Get staff permissions (staff)]]: Staff-only route that shows one staff member's permissions.
- [[Staff-Set-staff-permissions|Set staff permissions (staff)]]: Staff-only route that changes one staff member's permissions.
