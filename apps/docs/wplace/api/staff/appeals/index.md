---
title: "Staff: appeals"
description: Staff-only routes for the ban appeal queue.
---

These routes serve Wplace's staff dashboard, which ships inside the public client. They are listed so mod authors can recognise them in the bundle. Players get `401` or `403`. Their notes describe what each route is for, and nothing more.

- [[Staff-Take-the-next-appeal|Take the next appeal (staff)]]: Staff-only route that hands a moderator the next open ban appeal.
- [[Staff-Count-open-appeals|Count open appeals (staff)]]: Staff-only route that counts ban appeals waiting for a moderator.
- [[Staff-List-appeals|List appeals (staff)]]: Staff-only route that lists ban appeals.
- [[Staff-Assign-an-appeal|Assign an appeal (staff)]]: Staff-only route that assigns a ban appeal to a moderator.
- [[Staff-Decide-an-appeal|Decide an appeal (staff)]]: Staff-only route that accepts or rejects a ban appeal.
- [[Staff-Get-appeal-notes|Get appeal notes (staff)]]: Staff-only route that shows staff notes on a player's appeals.
- [[Staff-List-appeals-in-a-range|List appeals in a range (staff)]]: Staff-only route that lists ban appeals sent in a time range.
