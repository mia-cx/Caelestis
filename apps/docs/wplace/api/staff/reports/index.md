---
title: "Staff: metrics and logs"
description: Staff-only routes for metrics, audit logs and business accounts.
---

These routes serve Wplace's staff dashboard, which ships inside the public client. They are listed so mod authors can recognise them in the bundle. Players get `401` or `403`. Their notes describe what each route is for, and nothing more.

- [[Staff-Get-player-metric-options|Get player metric options (staff)]]: Staff-only route that lists the metrics the player dashboard can chart.
- [[Staff-Query-player-metrics|Query player metrics (staff)]]: Staff-only route that runs a player metrics query.
- [[Staff-List-metric-dashboards|List metric dashboards (staff)]]: Staff-only route that lists saved player metric dashboards.
- [[Staff-Create-a-metric-dashboard|Create a metric dashboard (staff)]]: Staff-only route that saves a player metric dashboard.
- [[Staff-Edit-a-metric-dashboard|Edit a metric dashboard (staff)]]: Staff-only route that changes a saved player metric dashboard.
- [[Staff-Delete-a-metric-dashboard|Delete a metric dashboard (staff)]]: Staff-only route that deletes a saved player metric dashboard.
- [[Staff-Set-the-default-dashboard|Set the default dashboard (staff)]]: Staff-only route that picks the default player metric dashboard.
- [[Staff-Get-revenue-metrics|Get revenue metrics (staff)]]: Staff-only route that charts revenue, top spenders and exchange rates.
- [[Staff-Set-a-revenue-target|Set a revenue target (staff)]]: Staff-only route that sets the revenue target for a period.
- [[Staff-Delete-a-revenue-target|Delete a revenue target (staff)]]: Staff-only route that deletes the revenue target for a period.
- [[Staff-Export-revenue|Export revenue (staff)]]: Staff-only route that downloads revenue data as a file.
- [[Staff-Get-ticket-metrics|Get ticket metrics (staff)]]: Staff-only route that charts report and ticket volume.
- [[Staff-Get-onboarding-metrics|Get onboarding metrics (staff)]]: Staff-only route that charts onboarding experiment results.
- [[Staff-Get-notice-metrics|Get notice metrics (staff)]]: Staff-only route that charts how players answer site notices.
- [[Staff-Read-the-audit-log|Read the audit log (staff)]]: Staff-only route that lists staff actions in the audit log.
- [[Staff-List-viewed-pages|List viewed pages (staff)]]: Staff-only route that lists which staff viewed which records.
- [[Staff-Get-a-page-view-trace|Get a page view trace (staff)]]: Staff-only route that shows one staff page view.
- [[Staff-List-view-log-events|List view log events (staff)]]: Staff-only route that lists events in the staff view log.
- [[Staff-List-businesses|List businesses (staff)]]: Staff-only route that lists business accounts.
- [[Staff-Get-a-business|Get a business (staff)]]: Staff-only route that shows one business account.
- [[Staff-Convert-to-a-business|Convert to a business (staff)]]: Staff-only route that turns an account into a business account.
- [[Staff-Add-a-business-employee|Add a business employee (staff)]]: Staff-only route that adds an employee to a business account.
- [[Staff-Remove-a-business-employee|Remove a business employee (staff)]]: Staff-only route that removes an employee from a business account.
- [[Staff-Set-business-charges|Set business charges (staff)]]: Staff-only route that changes a business account's paint charges.
- [[Staff-Set-business-paint-areas|Set business paint areas (staff)]]: Staff-only route that limits where a business account may paint.
