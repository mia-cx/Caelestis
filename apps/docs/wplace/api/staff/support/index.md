---
title: "Staff: support"
description: Staff-only routes for the support ticket queue and its routing.
---

These routes serve Wplace's staff dashboard, which ships inside the public client. They are listed so mod authors can recognise them in the bundle. Players get `401` or `403`. Their notes describe what each route is for, and nothing more.

- [[Staff-List-support-tickets|List support tickets (staff)]]: Staff-only route that lists every player's support tickets.
- [[Staff-Get-a-support-ticket|Get a support ticket (staff)]]: Staff-only route that shows any support ticket.
- [[Staff-Act-on-a-support-ticket|Act on a support ticket (staff)]]: Staff-only route that replies to or closes any support ticket.
- [[Staff-Mark-a-support-ticket-read|Mark a support ticket read (staff)]]: Staff-only route that marks a support ticket read for staff.
- [[Staff-Set-ticket-preferences|Set ticket preferences (staff)]]: Staff-only route that sets a staff member's preferences on one ticket.
- [[Staff-List-ticket-recipients|List ticket recipients (staff)]]: Staff-only route that lists who gets updates on a support ticket.
- [[Staff-Get-a-ticket-attachment|Get a ticket attachment (staff)]]: Staff-only route that downloads an attachment on any ticket.
- [[Staff-List-support-lanes|List support lanes (staff)]]: Staff-only route that lists the queues support tickets go to.
- [[Staff-Get-support-availability|Get support availability (staff)]]: Staff-only route that shows whether the staff member takes tickets.
- [[Staff-Set-support-availability|Set support availability (staff)]]: Staff-only route that sets whether the staff member takes tickets.
- [[Staff-Get-ticket-routing|Get ticket routing (staff)]]: Staff-only route that shows how tickets route to lanes.
- [[Staff-Set-ticket-routing|Set ticket routing (staff)]]: Staff-only route that changes how tickets route to lanes.
- [[Staff-List-support-staff|List support staff (staff)]]: Staff-only route that lists support staff and their levels.
- [[Staff-Set-a-support-level|Set a support level (staff)]]: Staff-only route that changes one staff member's support level.
- [[Staff-Save-support-settings|Save support settings (staff)]]: Staff-only route that changes the support system's settings.
