---
title: "Staff: alliances"
description: Staff-only routes for alliance records, moderation and headquarters.
---

These routes serve Wplace's staff dashboard, which ships inside the public client. They are listed so mod authors can recognise them in the bundle. Players get `401` or `403`. Their notes describe what each route is for, and nothing more.

- [[Staff-Search-alliances|Search alliances (staff)]]: Staff-only route that searches every alliance, hidden ones included.
- [[Staff-Get-an-alliance-record|Get an alliance record (staff)]]: Staff-only route that shows an alliance's staff record.
- [[Staff-Get-a-full-alliance-record|Get a full alliance record (staff)]]: Staff-only route that shows an alliance with every private field.
- [[Staff-List-alliance-members|List alliance members (staff)]]: Staff-only route that lists an alliance's members with staff fields.
- [[Staff-Set-a-member-role|Set a member role (staff)]]: Staff-only route that changes a member's role in any alliance.
- [[Staff-Remove-a-member|Remove a member (staff)]]: Staff-only route that removes a player from any alliance.
- [[Staff-Set-an-alliance-leader|Set an alliance leader (staff)]]: Staff-only route that makes a member the leader of any alliance.
- [[Staff-Rename-an-alliance|Rename an alliance (staff)]]: Staff-only route that renames any alliance.
- [[Staff-Edit-an-alliance-description|Edit an alliance description (staff)]]: Staff-only route that replaces any alliance's description.
- [[Staff-Archive-an-alliance|Archive an alliance (staff)]]: Staff-only route that archives an alliance.
- [[Staff-Restore-an-alliance|Restore an alliance (staff)]]: Staff-only route that restores an archived alliance.
- [[Staff-Hide-an-alliance|Hide an alliance (staff)]]: Staff-only route that hides an alliance from public lists.
- [[Staff-Punish-alliance-members|Punish alliance members (staff)]]: Staff-only route that punishes many members of an alliance at once.
- [[Staff-Ban-every-member|Ban every member (staff)]]: Staff-only route that bans every member of an alliance.
- [[Staff-List-ban-targets|List ban targets (staff)]]: Staff-only route that lists members a mass ban would hit.
- [[Staff-List-alliance-awards|List alliance awards (staff)]]: Staff-only route that shows an alliance's awards with staff fields.
- [[Staff-List-alliance-templates|List alliance templates (staff)]]: Staff-only route that lists any alliance's templates.
- [[Staff-Get-alliance-history|Get alliance history (staff)]]: Staff-only route that shows an alliance's change history.
- [[Staff-List-alliance-revisions|List alliance revisions (staff)]]: Staff-only route that lists past names, descriptions and assets.
- [[Staff-Hide-an-alliance-revision|Hide an alliance revision (staff)]]: Staff-only route that hides a past alliance revision.
- [[Staff-List-alliance-assets|List alliance assets (staff)]]: Staff-only route that lists an alliance's picture and banner drafts.
- [[Staff-Get-asset-draft-history|Get asset draft history (staff)]]: Staff-only route that replays who painted an alliance asset draft.
- [[Staff-Moderate-an-asset-draft|Moderate an asset draft (staff)]]: Staff-only route that removes or restores an alliance asset draft.
- [[Staff-Moderate-an-asset-version|Moderate an asset version (staff)]]: Staff-only route that removes or restores a published alliance asset.
- [[Staff-Inspect-a-headquarters-area|Inspect a headquarters area (staff)]]: Staff-only route that shows who painted an area of a headquarters.
- [[Staff-Get-headquarters-history|Get headquarters history (staff)]]: Staff-only route that lists paint events on a headquarters.
- [[Staff-Rewind-a-headquarters|Rewind a headquarters (staff)]]: Staff-only route that shows a headquarters canvas at an earlier time.
- [[Staff-Reverse-headquarters-paint|Reverse headquarters paint (staff)]]: Staff-only route that undoes paint on a headquarters.
- [[Staff-Moderate-a-headquarters|Moderate a headquarters (staff)]]: Staff-only route that locks or hides a headquarters.
- [[Staff-Get-headquarters-report-evidence|Get headquarters report evidence (staff)]]: Staff-only route that shows the evidence attached to a headquarters report.
- [[Staff-Get-alliance-report-evidence|Get alliance report evidence (staff)]]: Staff-only route that shows the evidence attached to an alliance report.
