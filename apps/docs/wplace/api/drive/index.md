The client can keep templates in the player's Google Drive.

- [[Get-drive-status|Get Google Drive status]]: Check whether the player linked Google Drive for saving templates.
- [[Connect-drive|Link Google Drive]]: Link Google Drive with an OAuth code.
- [[Get-drive-token|Get a Google Drive token]]: Get a short-lived Google Drive access token for the player.
- [[Disconnect-drive|Unlink Google Drive]]: Remove the player's Google Drive link.
