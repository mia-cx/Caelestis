The support ticket system, as players see it.

- [[Get-support-config|Get support settings]]: Get the support form's categories and limits.
- [[Get-support-permissions|Get my support permissions]]: Get what the signed-in player may do in support.
- [[Get-support-context|Get support context]]: Get account details the support form fills in for a new ticket.
- [[Get-support-terms-history|Get the support terms history]]: Get the current support terms and a list of every published version, for the support form and terms page.
- [[Get-support-term|Get one support terms version]]: Get one published version of the support terms, with its dates and full text.
- [[List-support-terms|Get the current support terms]]: Get the support terms in force now, with their version, dates and full text.
- [[List-support-cases|List my support tickets]]: List the signed-in player's support tickets.
- [[Get-support-case|Get a support ticket]]: Get one of the signed-in player's support tickets with its messages.
- [[Create-support-case|Open a support ticket]]: Open a new support ticket, with optional attachments.
- [[Act-on-support-case|Reply to a support ticket]]: Reply to or act on one of the player's support tickets.
- [[Mark-support-case-read|Mark a ticket read]]: Mark a support ticket as read up to a version.
- [[Get-support-attachment|Get a ticket attachment]]: Download one attachment of a support ticket.
- [[Support-stream|Stream support updates]]: Receive support ticket changes as server-sent events while the page is open.
