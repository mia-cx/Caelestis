---
title: My account
description: The signed-in player's account, settings, profile, favourites and sign-in.
order: 6
---

Endpoints under `/me` act on the signed-in player. [[Get-me]] is the one most mods care about.

- [Profile and cosmetics](profile/): Profile pictures, flags, frames, badges and name cosmetics the player owns.
- [Favourite places](favorites/): Save, edit, reorder and delete the player's favourite map locations.
- [Sign-in and linked accounts](sign-in/): Sign in, sign out, and link or unlink Discord.

- [[Get-me|Get my account]]: Get the signed-in player's account: charges, droplets, owned colours and profile.
- [[Get-my-email|Get my email]]: Get the email address on the signed-in player's account.
- [[Update-me|Update my profile]]: Change the signed-in player's display name and other profile fields.
- [[Delete-me|Delete my account]]: Delete the signed-in player's account after they type their name to confirm.
- [[Sign-out-everywhere|Sign out everywhere]]: End every session of the signed-in player's account.
- [[Get-my-suspension|Get my suspension]]: Get the bans and timeouts active on the signed-in player's account.
- [[Get-my-last-appeal|Get my last appeal date]]: Get when the signed-in player last appealed a ban.
- [[Get-my-pixels-today|Get my pixels today]]: Get how many pixels the signed-in player painted today.
- [[Get-account-conflict|Get an account conflict]]: Check whether the signed-in player's sign-in matches two accounts that need merging.
- [[Resolve-account-conflict|Resolve an account conflict]]: Pick which of two conflicting accounts to keep.
- [[Mark-rules-read|Mark the rules as read]]: Record that the signed-in player accepted the rules.
- [[Mark-store-seen|Mark the store as seen]]: Clear the "new in store" badge for the signed-in player.
- [[Update-privacy|Update privacy settings]]: Save the four privacy settings: last pixel, Discord, hotspots and headquarters pins.
- [[Get-control-bindings|Get my key bindings]]: Get the signed-in player's saved keyboard and mouse bindings.
- [[Save-control-bindings|Save my key bindings]]: Save the signed-in player's keyboard and mouse bindings to their account.
- [[Get-player-settings|Get my settings]]: Get the signed-in player's saved interface settings.
- [[Save-player-settings|Save my settings]]: Save the signed-in player's interface settings to their account.
