Place an image on the world map and paint your first pixels. You need [Caelestis](Install) and an image. Local templates work without a server.

1. Open the Caelestis moon on Wplace. Right-click **Local** and choose **Create from image**.
2. Select **Choose file** and open the image. The [template studio](Template-studio) previews it over the map.
3. Drag the artwork into position. Use **Size** to set its dimensions in map pixels.
4. Enter a name in **Template name** and click **Save**. The template appears under **Local**. Its eye button shows or hides the overlay.

![A placed template beside its entry under Local.](images/getting-started/placed-template.png)

## Paint a pixel

1. Zoom in until you can see individual pixels. Open Wplace's paint drawer and select a colour.
2. Place a draft pixel where the template asks for that colour. Submit your draft with Wplace's paint button.

![Strawberry artwork above the paint drawer with Red selected.](images/painting/select-colour.png)

Use [markers](Overlay-and-painting) to find unfinished pixels. To import artwork that already has a map position, follow [Templates](Templates).
