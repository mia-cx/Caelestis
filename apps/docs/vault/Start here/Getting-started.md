title: Getting started
---

This guide places a local template on Wplace. You need the [userscript installed](Install-the-userscript) and an image to paint. You do not need a Caelestis server.

## 1. Import your artwork

Open the Caelestis panel from Wplace's map controls. Hover the **Local** heading and click its import icon, outlined below. The **Import a template** button below the list does the same thing. Choose your file.

Caelestis accepts PNG images, Blue Marble exports, and native `.wplace` files.

![The Import a template control in the Local section.](images/getting-started/import-template.png)

## 2. Place it on the map

For an image without a saved position, move the artwork to where you want to paint. Use the blue checkmark to confirm the position. The **×** cancels placement.

![Artwork in placement mode, with the confirm and cancel controls visible.](images/getting-started/placement-controls.png)

## 3. Check the overlay

Your template now appears under **Local**. Zoom in until individual map pixels are visible. Check that the artwork sits where you intended.

The eye beside a template shows or hides its overlay. Use the template's actions menu to move it again.

![The placed Strawberry template and its row under Local.](images/getting-started/placed-template.png)

## 4. Choose a colour and paint

Select a colour in Wplace's palette. Caelestis marks remaining work for that colour. Paint through Wplace as usual.

Press `Shift+/` to see the current keyboard shortcuts. The [painting guide](Overlay-and-painting) explains colour navigation and marker settings.

![The Strawberry template above Wplace's paint drawer, with Red selected.](images/painting/select-colour.png)
