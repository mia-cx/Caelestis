Click the moon on Wplace to open your template library. The extension and userscript use the same panel.

| Control | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Local** | [Create](Template-studio), import and organise artwork in this browser. |
| Server name | Browse a [connected server's](Connect-to-a-server) shared templates. Expand a row to inspect progress. |
| **Reload tiles** | Refresh the tiles under your templates, including artwork outside the view. |
| **Pop out menu** | Open a larger library with a preview grid. **Return to sidebar** docks it again. |
| **Appearance** | Change [pixels, outlines and markers](Overlay-and-painting). |
| **Settings** | Manage connections, sharing, notifications and [keyboard shortcuts](Settings-and-shortcuts). |

![The panel header: the Caelestis moon and title, then Reload tiles and Pop out menu, then Appearance and the outlined Settings button, then Close, above the template search.](images/panel/panel-header.png)

![The popped-out library in preview grid view, with folder cards and template previews.](images/panel/popout-preview-grid.png)

The map rail holds painter, claim and overlay controls. Open **More Caelestis controls** when the rail runs out of room. Press `Shift+/` for the keyboard reference. Hover a key to read its action and see your current bindings.
