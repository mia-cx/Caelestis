---
title: Alliance canvases
---

Place a template inside your alliance headquarters, avatar or banner. Open the editor for the artwork you want to paint. Use the Caelestis controls beside that canvas.

1. Open the template panel inside the editor. Its local and server folders belong to the active canvas and alliance.
2. Choose **Create from image** in the destination folder. Drop in an image, then crop, resize and position it with the [template studio](Template-studio). Use image files here. Blue Marble and native world-map files carry world coordinates.
3. Click **Save**. Select a colour in the alliance paint palette. The markers and progress count the artwork on this canvas, including your unsubmitted draft.
4. Hold Shift while painting to place only unfinished pixels that need the selected colour. Use the template display controls to change its overlay or peek at the artwork underneath.

The editor keeps navigation and painting shortcuts on the active canvas. It also has its own pixel, marker and colour settings. If the canvas is too small for the controls, enlarge the editor.

You can [export the artwork](Templates#move-replace-or-export-artwork) from the row menu. For the differences between alliance and world painting controls, see [Dangerous settings](Settings-and-shortcuts#dangerous-settings).
