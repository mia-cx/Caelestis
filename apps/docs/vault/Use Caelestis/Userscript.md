The userscript adds Caelestis to Wplace. Use the panel to manage templates and settings, and the map controls while painting.

If this is your first visit, [install the userscript](Install-the-userscript) and [place a template](Getting-started).

## Find a control

| Where | What is there |
| --- | --- |
| Template panel | Your template tree, search, filters, sorting, and template actions. |
| Panel header | Popout, Appearance, and Settings. |
| Map button rail | Painting, overlay, painter, and claim controls. Hover a button to read its label. |
| `Shift+/` | Keyboard reference showing your current bindings. There is no keyboard-reference button on the rail. |

## Where templates live

**Local** templates stay in this browser. Export anything you want to keep before clearing browser storage.

**Server** templates belong to the connected server. Other people with access to that server can use them too.

Caelestis also reads Wplace's **personal** and **alliance** templates. These remain separate from Caelestis local and server storage.

## Sharing

You choose whether to report painting, share fetched tiles, and show your viewport or drafts to other painters. These are separate switches in [Settings](Settings-and-shortcuts#sharing-and-painters).

Painting and tile reports go only to servers whose templates cover that part of the map.
